# Maoxuan Product Agent

**Maoxuan Product Agent** is a Chinese-first product decision Agent Skill distilled from the complete reasoning
structures of *On Contradiction* and *On Practice* for modern internet product work.

## Tags

🛠️ Productivity Tools | ✅ Verified

## Core Philosophy

- **Distill methods, not quotations**: The source methodology stays in the background; answers use modern product
  language and do not discuss politics, history, or philosophy by default.
- **Find the current bottleneck**: Diagnose the product stage, evidence, constraints, stakeholders, and mechanism
  before recommending tactics.
- **Return decisions to practice**: Turn uncertain judgments into minimum diagnostics, reversible tests, rollout
  criteria, and explicit stop conditions.

## Key Features & Workflow

1. **Problem diagnosis**: Separates observable facts, hypotheses, second-hand judgments, and isolated feedback before
   defining the real problem.
2. **Stage-sensitive prioritization**: Identifies which constraint currently controls the outcome and which work
   should be deferred.
3. **Concrete next actions**: Produces one to three actions with an owner, time window, metric, or decision rule
   instead of a long list of generic advice.
4. **Broad product coverage**: Includes 36 playbooks for prioritization, Roadmaps, growth, retention, conversion,
   operations, data anomalies, A/B tests, delivery, and cross-functional collaboration.
5. **Chinese workplace output**: Defaults to concise Simplified Chinese while retaining familiar product terms such
   as DAU, GMV, MVP, ROI, OKR, and A/B Test.

## Skills Library Overview

- **Decision workflow**: `SKILL.md` — trigger rules, internal reasoning sequence, output contract, and quality
  boundaries.
- **Reasoning engine**: `references/reasoning-engine.md` — stage, bottleneck, mechanism, evidence, and validation
  logic.
- **Product playbooks**: `references/product-playbooks.md` — 36 recurring product, growth, operations, data, and
  collaboration scenarios.
- **Quality assurance**: Automated quality gates plus documented self-tests and clean-session forward tests.
