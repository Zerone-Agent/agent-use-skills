# Aaron Marketing Skills

**Aaron Marketing Skills** is a 69-skill marketing operator for AI agents, covering four disciplines — SEO/GEO, influencer marketing (IMPACT), paid ads (ROAS), and email marketing (SEND) — on one shared operating contract, with five benchmark-driven auditor gates and zero-dependency keyless data connectors.

📈 Marketing & Growth | ✅ Verified

## Core Philosophy

- **One contract, 69 skills**: every skill exposes the same sections (trigger → quick start → skill contract → handoff summary → next best skill), so the bundle behaves like one operating system — each skill knows its inputs, outputs, and the next skill to hand off to.
- **Gated quality, not vibes**: five benchmarks (CORE-EEAT, CITE, C³, ROAS, SEND) drive five auditor gates that emit machine-checkable SHIP / FIX / BLOCK verdicts with veto rules.
- **Keyless by default**: every skill runs Tier-1 on pasted data; optional Python-stdlib connectors pull free/own data (Search Console, GA4, Wikipedia attention, DNS email-auth records, YouTube metrics, and more) with no paid tools.
- **Truth lives in registries**: brand entities, creator dossiers, offer/claim substantiation, and consent records live in dedicated protocol-layer registries that the gates judge against.

## Key Features & Workflow

1. **SEO/GEO (16 skills)** — keyword/SERP/gap research, content drafting, GEO citation-readiness, schema, programmatic/parasite/local plays, then CORE-EEAT and CITE audits plus rank/off-site monitoring.
2. **Influencer · IMPACT (16)** — audience/niche mapping, creator discovery and fit scoring, campaign planning and briefs, outreach, the C³ ART compliance gate, amplification, and ROI measurement.
3. **Paid ads · ROAS (16)** — campaign architecture, segments, creative and experiments, the RQS launch gate with conversion-signal QA, then attribution reconciliation, pacing, and fatigue control.
4. **Email · SEND (16)** — deliverability/auth pre-flight, list growth and hygiene, creative and rendering, lifecycle sequences, newsletter monetization, the EQS pre-send gate, and inbox-placement monitoring.
5. **Protocol layer (5)** — entity/creator/claims/consent truth registries plus a HOT/WARM/COLD cross-session memory loop.
